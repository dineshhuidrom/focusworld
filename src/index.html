    About {{APP_NAME}}

    {{APP_NAME}} is a free focus timer for studying and work. Pick a time, press start, and focus. Every minute you focus grows your own little world: grass, trees, a cottage, a windmill, a lighthouse and more. 🌱

    Is it really free?

    Yes! No sign-up, no payment. The Toolshed has a few handy helpers. Some are free, some are paid, and they help keep {{APP_NAME}} free for everyone. Paid promotions always show an “Ad” label.

    How does my world grow?

    Every focused minute counts. 31 things unlock as your minutes add up, from a first sprout all the way to a whale. Your garden also visits a new place every day: a mountain valley, a desert oasis, a city rooftop, an underwater dome, a space garden and more. Focus more to unlock them all.

    Which timer lengths can I use?

    15, 25, 50 or 90 minutes, or any time from 1 to 180 minutes, with short breaks in between. Can’t get started? Tap “2 tiny minutes”. Starting is the hardest part!

    Is it good for the Pomodoro technique?

    Yes. The classic Pomodoro is 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break. {{APP_NAME}} can start your breaks by itself, and your streak grows every day you focus.

    Can I study with music?

    Yes: soft rain, ocean waves, forest birds, a crackling fire, brown noise and calm lofi music. Mix them however you like in Sounds. They play while you focus and stop when the timer ends; tap 🔊 next to Pause to stop or play them. Breaks are quiet unless you switch on “Play during breaks” in Sounds. They are made right on your device, so they work offline too.

    Where is my world saved?

    Only on this device, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. To move it to another phone or laptop, use “Save backup” in Settings. Clearing your browser data erases it, so save a backup now and then.

    Does it work offline? Can I install it?

    After your first visit it works offline. On your phone, use “Add to Home Screen” to keep it like an app.

    Do you collect my details?

    No sign-up, ever. A few products show a short “Before you go” form (your name, WhatsApp number or email) so {{APP_NAME}} can help you with them. You can always tap Skip. Privacy

    What is phone-free mode?

    An optional challenge for big study days. If you leave the page for more than 10 seconds while focusing, your sprout droops. Turn it on in Settings.
