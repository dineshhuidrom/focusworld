Privacy

Updated {{UPDATED}}

Short version

No sign-up and no ads. Your island, timer and settings stay on your own device. We only keep your details (name, WhatsApp number or email) if you choose to fill the “Before you go” form, and only so {{APP_NAME}} can help you.

What stays on your device

Your island, your focus minutes, your streak, your settings, and what you type to Pip (the helper) are saved only in your browser, on your device. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data erases them.

The “Before you go” form

Some products show a short form before their link opens. If you fill it, we keep:

  • what you typed: your name, WhatsApp number and/or email (each product asks only for what it needs),
  • which product you opened, and the time.

Why: so {{APP_NAME}} can contact you about that product. Who sees it: only the owner of {{APP_NAME}}. It is never sold or shared. Where: a private database on Cloudflare, the company that hosts this site. How long: it is deleted by itself after 12 months, or sooner when you ask.

You never have to fill it: tap Skip and the link opens anyway.

To stop one person sending the form again and again, we keep a code made from your network address. The code changes every day, and your network address itself is not stored.

Links to other sites

Products open other websites (for example Gumroad, WhatsApp, YouTube or Instagram). Those sites have their own privacy rules.
